## Successes

- Three distinct operator complaints — cramped lyric slides, missing chorus repetition, redundant title slides during prayer — were closed in one narrow story without touching `ParsedRundown` or the web form.
- Continuous-text lyric joining is punctuation-aware (terminal punctuation joins with a space, otherwise `"; "`), so readability improved without a manual per-hymn pass.
- The title-skip rules were scoped to three named positions (intercessory 671, intercessory 684, We Have This Hope) rather than a blanket rule, which kept the change reversible and testable.
- `tests/lyrics.test.mjs` and `tests/slide-plan.test.mjs` pinned the new behaviour, including the exact first/last lines of the two "We Have This Hope" slides. Those assertions held unchanged through the Epic 16 rewrite of the entire planner — the single strongest signal that this story's tests were written at the right altitude.

## Challenges

- The story arrived as a free-form Cursor handover with prose rules rather than Given/When/Then acceptance criteria; correctness had to be inferred from the narrative.
- Skip rules are keyed to hymn titles and fixed positions. That is fine today, but it encodes worship-flow knowledge in `slide-plan.ts` where a registry or configuration would be a better home.
- The retrospective was left in `backlog` while Epic 16 started, so this document is written after the fact.

## Key insights

1. **Narrow refinement stories are the cheapest kind of work in this repo.** One file pair, focused tests, no boundary change.
2. **Tests written against observable output survive refactors.** Epic 16 replaced the planner's entire internals and this epic's assertions never needed editing.
3. **Standing worship rules keep accumulating as literals.** Epic 16 moved standing *content* into the registry; standing *flow* rules (which titles to skip) are still hardcoded and are the natural next candidate.
