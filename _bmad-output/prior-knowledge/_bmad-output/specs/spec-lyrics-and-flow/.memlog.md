- **decision:** Implement continuous text formatting for verse lyrics. Replace `\n` with `; ` unless preceded by punctuation (where it becomes ` `).
- **decision:** Chorus must be injected after every verse for all songs, regardless of how it is stored in the database.
- **decision:** Skip song title slides for specific transitional parts (e.g. after intercessory prayer, "We Have This Hope").
- **capability (CAP-1):** Format verse lyrics into continuous paragraphs based on punctuation presence at line ends.
  - intent: Consolidate vertical space and improve readability of lyrics.
  - success: Lyrics like `Verse 1\nLine 1\nLine 2` become `Line 1; Line 2` (or `Line 1. Line 2` if punctuation exists).
- **capability (CAP-2):** Inject Chorus after every mapped Verse during slide generation.
  - intent: Automate chorus repetition so operators don't have to manually ensure the chorus is sung after every verse.
  - success: The slide plan sequence for any song with a Chorus becomes `Verse 1 -> Chorus -> Verse 2 -> Chorus` etc.
- **capability (CAP-3):** Skip specific song title slides during the intercessory prayer flow.
  - intent: Remove unnecessary transitional slides that disrupt the flow of the worship service.
  - success: Song title slides for "Now Dear Lord As We Pray", "Hear Our Prayer, O Lord", and "We Have This Hope" are explicitly filtered out from the slide plan.
- **constraint:** Long continuous verses should still split across multiple slides if they exceed optimal character limits, maintaining a readable presentation.
- **constraint:** The skip rules apply only to the generated slide plan (PPTX and Preview), not to the parsed rundown data structure.

- (capability) CAP-4: Format 'We Have This Hope' lyrics as 2 fixed slides. Intent: Maintain traditional poetic stanza pacing for the closing prayer song. Success: 'We Have This Hope' yields exactly 2 slides preserving manual line breaks, exempt from continuous prose joining.
